FAQ

Questions business owners ask.

Why should I register my business? +
Formal registration can give a business legal recognition and may be required for certain business activities, contracts and financial arrangements.
What is SCUML? +
SCUML is the Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering. Its requirements apply to specified designated non-financial businesses and professions.
Can you help me know what my business needs? +
A consultation can help clarify the registrations and compliance steps relevant to your particular business.
